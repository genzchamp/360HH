SHARIAH FRAMEWORK

Evidence, methodology and humility.

360HH will distinguish documented principles from scholarly disagreement. It will not turn complex fiqh questions into a simplistic green “halal” button.

FOUNDATION

Riba

Interest and prohibited interest-based structures must be identified rather than hidden behind product names.

FOUNDATION

Gharar & maysir

Educational material will examine excessive uncertainty, gambling-like structures and transactions that depend on prohibited mechanisms.

SHARES

Business activity

Company activity matters. Shariah analysis must examine what the company actually does, alongside the applicable financial criteria.

CRYPTO

Open scholarly questions

The International Islamic Fiqh Academy has highlighted unresolved questions about cryptocurrency's nature, value and tradability and recommended further research.

PRODUCTS

High-risk structures

Margin, interest-bearing products, derivatives, short selling and similar structures require specific contract-level analysis rather than a generic asset label.

GOVERNANCE

Source trail

Where possible, 360HH will link readers to primary institutional sources and state when a conclusion is disputed or requires qualified scholarly review.

Important: This platform is educational and is not a personal fatwa, investment recommendation or guarantee of Shariah compliance. Shariah views can differ; personal rulings should be taken to a qualified scholar who understands the relevant transaction.
