01 · RIBA

Understanding and avoiding riba

Riba is a foundational prohibition in Islamic commercial law. The site should teach the principle from revelation first, then show how contemporary products can contain interest or interest-like structures.

What the Qur'an says

Allah says in Qur'an 2:275 that He has permitted trade and forbidden riba. Qur'an 2:278-279 commands believers to give up what remains of riba and states that repentance leaves the principal amount, without wronging or being wronged. These verses are foundational texts for the prohibition.

Qur'an 2:275Qur'an 2:278–279

Common modern examples to investigate

Interest-bearing loans, conventional interest on balances, certain bonds, margin borrowing and products where the lender is contractually entitled to a predetermined increase for the loan require careful Shariah analysis. Do not assume a product is permissible because the provider calls it “yield,” “APR,” “bonus” or “financing.”

Trade is not automatically riba

The Qur'an itself distinguishes trade from riba. A genuine sale has an underlying asset/benefit and contractual structure; a loan with an interest increase is a different relationship. The exact contract matters.

Hadith evidence

Sahih Muslim 1598 reports the Prophet ﷺ condemning the one who consumes riba, the one who pays it, the one who records it and the two witnesses, describing them as equal in that transaction.

Sahih Muslim 1598 →

Dua for guidance and protection

One may sincerely ask Allah for guidance, taqwa and lawful provision. A rigorously sourced general supplication is:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْهُدَى وَالتُّقَى وَالْعَفَافَ وَالْغِنَى

Allahumma inni as'alukal-huda wat-tuqa wal-'afafa wal-ghina — “O Allah, I ask You for guidance, piety, chastity and sufficiency.”

This is a general dua for guidance and sufficiency, not a claim that the Prophet ﷺ prescribed this exact wording specifically as a “riba dua.”

02 · GHARAR

Gharar: clarity in contracts

Gharar concerns prohibited uncertainty or ambiguity in a transaction. Islamic commercial law does not require every outcome to be certain; rather, the nature of the contractual uncertainty and its materiality matter.

What to examine

Ask: What is being sold? Who owns it? What exactly is the price? What are the delivery terms? What happens if delivery fails? What rights does each party receive? Are material terms hidden or unknowable?

Hadith

Sahih Muslim 1513 reports that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ prohibited the sale involving gharar. This hadith is a major primary text used in discussions of excessive contractual uncertainty.

Sahih Muslim 1513 →

Gharar is not “all risk”

Business naturally contains uncertainty and market risk. The concept should not be reduced to “anything risky is gharar.” Scholars analyze the type, degree and contractual location of uncertainty.

Practical habit

Before entering a transaction, rewrite the contract in plain language. If you cannot explain what you own, what you owe, what the other party owes, and what happens in failure scenarios, stop and investigate before proceeding.

03 · MAYSIR

Maysir: gambling and wager-like structures

Maysir is explicitly prohibited. The Qur'an instructs believers to avoid gambling and identifies harms including enmity and distraction from remembrance of Allah and prayer.

Qur'an 5:90–91

Qur'an 5:90 commands believers to avoid maysir, and 5:91 explains some of its social and spiritual harms. This is why 360HH does not frame gambling as a legitimate “investment strategy.”

Qur'an 5:90–91

Research the economic substance

Ask whether participants are genuinely buying/owning an asset or service, or whether the primary mechanism is wagering money on an uncertain event for the possibility of winning another party's money.

Do not confuse volatility with gambling

A risky investment is not automatically maysir. The analysis depends on ownership, contract, purpose, consideration and how the return is generated.

Trading psychology

Even where a transaction is structurally permissible, reckless behavior can become harmful. 360HH promotes planning, evidence and discipline rather than emotional chasing.

04 · BUSINESS ACTIVITIES

What does the company actually do?

For shares, the underlying business matters. A Shariah review should investigate the company's main activities, revenue sources and financial structure rather than relying on a sector label alone.

Core activity

Identify the products and services. Investigate prohibited lines of business and whether they are central or incidental. Use current company filings and primary disclosures where possible.

Financial structure

For a mixed company, relevant Shariah screening methodology may examine debt, cash, interest income and the composition of assets. Exact thresholds and methodologies should be attributed to the standard or screening methodology being used.

AAOIFI Standard 21

AAOIFI Shari'ah Standard No. 21 provides detailed rules concerning financial papers including shares and bonds. It distinguishes situations by the nature and composition of the company's assets and activity.

Read AAOIFI SS 21 →
Important: A screening result is not permanent. Company activities, financial ratios and standards can change. Record the data date and methodology used.
05 · CRYPTO

Crypto: evidence, uncertainty and contract-level analysis

Crypto requires particular humility because the category includes radically different assets and products, and major contemporary institutions have identified unresolved questions.

What IIFA Resolution 237 says

The International Islamic Fiqh Academy reviewed digital currencies and recorded unresolved questions about whether a cryptocurrency is a commodity, benefit, investment asset, digital asset or other form of value recognized in Shariah, alongside significant risks.

Read IIFA Resolution 237 →

Bitcoin is not every crypto product

Even if someone forms a view about a particular coin, that does not automatically answer questions about staking, lending, perpetuals, margin, options, wrapped assets, stablecoins or exchange products.

How 360HH should present crypto

Use “research status” rather than a simplistic halal score. State the asset, product, source, date, unresolved issues and scholarly disagreement.

06 · FINANCIAL PRODUCTS

Analyse the contract, not the marketing

Margin & leverage

Ask where the financing comes from, whether interest is charged, what collateral rights exist, what happens on liquidation and whether the structure combines contracts in a prohibited way.

Short selling

Short selling involves specific questions about ownership, borrowing and selling an asset one does not own. It requires its own fiqh analysis and should not be bundled into ordinary share ownership.

Derivatives

Futures, options, perpetual contracts and CFDs can have complex questions involving ownership, settlement, speculation, uncertainty, leverage and contract structure. They require product-specific review.

Yield & staking

“Yield” is not a Shariah category. Determine whether the return comes from a genuine service, profit-sharing, asset use, lending, interest, incentives or another mechanism.

07 · GOVERNANCE

How 360HH handles Shariah research

Primary sources first

Prefer Qur'an and authentic Sunnah for foundational texts, then recognized contemporary fiqh institutions and standards for modern financial structures.

Attribution

When a standard gives a methodology, name the standard. When scholars disagree, identify the disagreement. Do not convert one methodology into “Islam says” without qualification.

Date the evidence

Financial data and products change. Every screening or research note should record when the information was reviewed.

Scholar review

Complex personal transactions should go to a qualified scholar who understands contemporary financial contracts. A website cannot replace a personal fatwa.

Trusted starting points
