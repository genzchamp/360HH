THE 360 APPROACH

From curiosity to capability.

Financial content should do more than make you excited about an asset. 360HH is being built around a simple sequence: learn → verify → calculate → reflect → decide.

That means separating education from hype, calculations from predictions, and documented Shariah positions from areas where qualified scholars continue to differ.

Some questions need nuance.

For stocks, Islamic finance standards provide structured principles for permissible activities and trading. Crypto is more unsettled: the International Islamic Fiqh Academy has identified unresolved questions around its nature, value and tradability and called for further research.
